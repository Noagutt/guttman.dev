autoReminder

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

autoReminder is software made by גוטמן נעה ("we"). Offices (our customers) use it to send their own clients WhatsApp reminders about documents. This policy explains what data the software handles and how.

Who controls the data

Each office runs its own install of autoReminder. The office decides which clients to remind and owns their data. We act on the office's behalf and do not use client data for any other purpose.

What data is processed

  • Client name, phone number and the reminder groups the office assigns.
  • Which documents are required and which have been received.
  • Message delivery status (sent, delivered, read, failed) returned by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Documents that clients send in reply, when the office enables document collection.

How it is used

Only to send the office's reminders, track missing documents, and show the office delivery status. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except Meta, which delivers the WhatsApp messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored in the office's own install (its computer or the cloud account it chooses). The office decides how long to keep it and can delete it at any time.

Your choices

Clients who no longer want reminders can tell the office, or reply to the message, and the office will stop them. To ask about, correct or delete your data, contact the office that messaged you, or us at noa@guttman.dev.
